# Giftiary – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the Giftiary Android app and its service handle personal data. A Hungarian version with the same content is also available.

## 1. Controller and contact

- **Controller:** Tímea Antal
- **Email (including privacy matters):** support@giftiary.app
- **Website:** https://giftiary.app

Giftiary is operated by a private individual. No data protection officer is required. Please send any privacy question or request to the email address above.

## 2. In short

- Giftiary helps you remember, plan and share gifts. You can record people, occasions, gift ideas, gifts and wishlists.
- Your data is first stored on your device and then synced to our server under your account, so that you can use it on more than one device.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, tracking or advertising identifiers, and we do **not** collect location, contacts or files from your device. We process a photo only if you add one yourself: to a gift, to a person, or as your own profile photo. Every photo is private to your account in this release. There are no payments in the app.
- We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account), or ask for its deletion by email without the app (see section 12).

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1. Account data

- **Email address:** needed to register and sign in.
- **Password:** if you register with email and password. Our authentication provider stores it only in a secure, irreversible (hashed) form; the app does not store it.
- **Account ID:** a random technical identifier your data is linked to.
- **Account status:** for example when you registered and confirmed your email, and when you last signed in.

### 3.2. Signing in with Google or Facebook (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook", you sign in on the provider's own page in a browser; we never see your Google or Facebook password. Our authentication system receives from the provider, and stores with your account:

- your email address (if the provider shares it) and whether it is verified;
- the name you use with the provider and a link (URL) to your profile picture;
- your account ID at the provider.

We request only basic sign-in access (email and basic profile). Giftiary does **not display or use** the name or picture received from the provider, and does not fill your display name with it. We have no access to your other Google or Facebook data, and the app contains no Google or Facebook SDK.

If you already have a Giftiary account with the same verified email address, the authentication provider may link the Google or Facebook sign-in to that existing account.

### 3.3. Profile

- **Display name (optional):** you can set it in your profile (Edit profile). It is visible only to people you share a wishlist with, or whose shared list you have joined. Without a display name, a generic label such as "A Giftiary member" is shown instead.
- **Profile photo (optional):** one image you choose with the system photo picker or take with your camera app, in Edit profile. We crop it to a square, resize it to 640 × 640 pixels, convert it to JPEG and remove its metadata. **Only you see it**: it is not shown to the people you share wishlists with, and it is not part of invites, memberships or shared lists. It is stored only on our server, not kept on your device; changing it needs an internet connection.

### 3.4. Content you enter

- **People:** name, relationship (e.g. "mother", "friend").
- **Occasions:** type (e.g. birthday, name day, anniversary, Christmas, custom), day, month, optionally year, and a title for custom occasions.
- **Gift ideas and gifts:** name, direction (given or received), who gave or received it, occasion, price or estimated price, product link, note, status.
- **Wishlists and wishes:** list name, wish name, note, estimated price, product link.
- **Gift photo (optional):** at most one image per gift, which you choose with the system photo picker or take with your device's camera app. Before it is stored we resize it (at most 1600 pixels on the longer side), convert it to JPEG and remove its metadata (for example location and device data).
- **Person photo (optional):** at most one image per person you record, chosen or taken the same way. We crop it to a square, resize it to 640 × 640 pixels, convert it to JPEG and remove its metadata. Such a photo usually shows someone else (see section 5).

You type this data in yourself; nothing is read automatically from your device (for example, not your contacts or your calendar). The photo picker gives the app only the one image you choose; we have no access to your other photos. If you take a new photo, your camera app saves it into a temporary file that Giftiary creates for this purpose only. Giftiary processes it as above and deletes the temporary file right after processing. If the app stops while you are taking the photo, the leftover temporary file is deleted after one day, the next time you take a photo (or earlier by the system when it clears the cache). Giftiary does not save the photo to your gallery; however, some camera apps may keep a copy according to their own settings, which you can check in your camera app.

### 3.5. Sharing data

- **Invites:** when you share one of your own wishlists, an invite is created. The server stores the invite's secret code only in an irreversible (hashed) form; the code itself appears only in the shared link. We also store when the invite was created, when it expires and, if applicable, when it was revoked.
- **Email invites:** you can also invite someone to one of your own wishlists by entering their email address. The server then stores the invited email address (lowercased, with surrounding spaces removed), the list's identifier, the invite's status (pending, accepted, declined, revoked or expired), and when the invite was created, when it expires (14 days after creation) and when it was closed. We use the email address only for this invitation: to send the invitee one invitation email (see section 3.7) and to show the invite in the app to someone who signs in with exactly that address as a confirmed email address, who can accept or decline it. Before accepting, they see the list's name, your display name and, if you keep the list for one of your people, that person's name. We do not use the address for advertising or any other purpose. The invite does not store the invited account's identifier, and we do not tell you whether the address belongs to a Giftiary account. Only you see the invited email address, among your pending invites. When the invite closes (it is accepted, declined, revoked or expires), we erase the email address from it; it then no longer appears among your invites.
- **Memberships:** who joined a list, and when.
- **Reservations:** which member reserved a wish.

### 3.6. Technical data

To keep sign-in and the service secure and working, our authentication and database provider processes technical data. Examples are session (sign-in) records, request times, the IP address and device/app identification data (user agent), and technical logs kept for a short period.

We have not built any analytics or crash-reporting tool into the app. Google Play may make aggregated technical statistics (such as crash rates) available to us under its own terms.

### 3.7. Emails

We send emails from no-reply@giftiary.app to confirm your registration and to reset a forgotten password. For this we process your email address, the content of the message (the confirmation or reset link) and technical delivery data. We do not send newsletters or marketing emails.

**Invitation email to an invited person (GDPR Article 14).** When a Giftiary user invites an email address to a wishlist, we send that address one email, in Hungarian and English. It says that a Giftiary user has invited them to a shared wishlist, without naming the user or the list; that if they already use Giftiary they can open the app, and otherwise register with this address within 14 days (by the date given) to see the invitation; that the address was entered by the person who invited them and is used only for this invitation, not for marketing; when the address is erased; the legal basis; how to object and exercise their rights; and a link to this privacy policy. The email is the same whether or not the address belongs to a Giftiary account. For a repeated invitation while one is still pending, no new email is sent. For this we process the invited email address, the invitation's expiry date and technical delivery data.

### 3.8. Data stored on your device

- A local copy of your data in the app's own database, so the app works offline.
- Local copies of your gift and person photos in the app's own storage, and the list of photos waiting to be uploaded or deleted from the file storage.
- A profile photo you have just picked, only until it is uploaded. If it cannot be uploaded and you do not try again, the app deletes it automatically later.
- While you take a photo, a temporary image file in the app's cache (see section 3.4).
- Your sign-in session (while you are signed in).
- Your reminder setting (on/off) and the day of the last reminder, per account.
- A marker that you have already seen the introduction screens on this device (per device, not tied to an account).
- A temporary marker if an account deletion has not finished on the device yet.

Android backup (if enabled on your device) does **not** include the session, the local database, photos or the deletion marker; it does include the reminder settings and the introduction marker. Backup is performed by Google under its own terms, according to your device settings.

### 3.9. If you contact us

If you email us, we process your message and your email address to answer your request.

## 5. Data about other people

In Giftiary you can record data about other people (for example their names, birthdays, gifts meant for them or received from them, and optionally a photo of them), and other people may also appear in gift photos. We process this data only to provide the service to you:
- only you can see it, except the content of shared wishlists (see section 7); a person's photo is never shown to anyone else;
- we do not contact or notify those people (except the invitation email of section 3.7, if you invite someone by their email address);
- we do not use their data for any other purpose.

Please record only what you need for your own personal gift planning, and add a photo of someone only if you may use it for this private purpose. If someone believes we process data about them in Giftiary, they can contact us using the details in section 18.

## 6. Storage and sync

Giftiary works offline: changes are saved on your device first and, when you are online, synced to the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. On the server, every account can access only its own data; database-level access control enforces this.

Gift and person photos are also saved on your device first and uploaded to a private file storage when you are online; the gift or person syncs with its photo only after the photo is uploaded. In the storage, every account can access only its own folder, photos have no public link, and your other devices download them from there when needed.

Your profile photo is not saved on your device first: it is uploaded straight away to a separate private file storage that only your account can access, and it counts as changed only once the upload has succeeded.

## 7. Wishlist sharing, invites and reservations

- You can share your own wishlist with an invite link. You pass the link on yourself, in any way you choose (for example in a messaging app). Anyone who receives the link can join the list while the invite is valid (currently 7 days), after signing in. You can revoke an invite at any time.
- **Members see:** the list name, the wishes (name, note, estimated price, product link) and the list owner's display name.
- **The list owner sees:** the members' display names and when they joined, the open invite links, and the pending email invites (with the invited address).
- **Reservations:** for each wish, members see only whether it is available, reserved by them or reserved by someone else. They do not see who reserved it. **The list owner sees nothing about reservations**, so the surprise is kept.
- The owner can remove a member, and a member can leave; the member's reservations then no longer count.
- Sharing gives access to that wishlist only. Your people (and their photos), occasions, gift ideas, gifts (and their photos), your profile photo and other lists do not become visible. This also holds for a list you keep for one of your people: members see that person's name as the list's recipient, never their photo.

## 8. Reminders and notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app builds the notification about upcoming occasions locally on your device. It may contain the names of people and the occasion. The notification is not sent by our server, and no data is transmitted for it. On the lock screen, the content is hidden, depending on your device settings. You can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

## 11. Retention

- **Account data and the content you enter:** for as long as your account exists, or until you delete them.
- **Deleted items:**
  - If you delete an item in the app (for example a person or a gift), it disappears from all views.
  - On the server it is kept, marked as deleted, so that your other devices learn of the deletion; the sync change log is kept too.
  - Both are kept until the account is deleted; there is currently no automatic permanent deletion.
- **Gift and person photos:**
  - If you replace or remove a photo, or delete the gift or person, the old photo's local copy on the device where you do it is deleted right away.
  - It is deleted from the file storage once the change has synced from that device; while the device is offline, the photo stays in the file storage.
  - On your other devices the local copy is deleted during the syncs after they receive the change; if that device downloaded the copy within the last day, up to about one day later. On a signed-out device this does not happen until the next sign-in.
  - Photos are not kept marked as deleted.
- **Profile photo:** if you replace or remove it, the previous photo is deleted from the file storage right after the change. If that deletion fails (for example the connection drops), the leftover is deleted at a later profile photo change, or with the account.
- **Expired and revoked invites:** kept as rows (with their expiry and revocation time) until the account is deleted.
- **Email invites:** the invited email address is kept only while the invite is pending, for at most 14 days. When the invite is accepted, declined or revoked, we erase the address at once; when it expires, an automatic job erases it within minutes of the expiry. The invite's row is then kept without the address (with the list, status and the times of creation, expiry and closing) until your account is deleted; deleting the invitee's account does not delete it. Email delivery data is kept by our email provider according to its settings.
- **Signing out:**
  - Signing out is not deletion. On the device, the account's data and its gift and person photos stay stored but hidden, so that you can reach it again when you sign back in with the same account.
  - To remove the data from the device as well, delete the account, or clear the app's data / uninstall the app.
- **Technical logs and email delivery data:** for a limited period, according to our providers' settings.
- **Backups:** backups made by our hosting provider may contain deleted data until they are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
- **Enquiries:** for as long as needed to answer and document the request.
- **Correspondence about closed deletion requests:** 90 days after a request is closed, we manually and permanently delete the full correspondence and its attachments from all folders of the mailboxes we manage and any additional copies under our control, including sent messages. This rule applies to the correspondence; it does not cover a separate record of the request's completion.
- **Deletion-request completion log:** we retain the minimum record needed to document completion for one year after the request is closed, then manually delete it. It contains the case ID, the affected account's UUID, the dates of receipt, confirmation, completion, closure and notification, the final check result, the deadlines for deleting the correspondence and log entry, and the actual correspondence deletion date. We do not copy email addresses, message bodies, photos or other user content into it. Access is restricted; we treat a log containing a UUID as personal data.

## 12. Deleting your account

**In the app:** Settings → Delete account, after a confirmation. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

**What happens:**

- **On the server**, first all of the account's gift and person photos, and then its profile photos, are deleted from the file storage; then, in a single step, the following are permanently deleted:
  - all of your account's people, occasions, gift ideas, gifts, wishlists and wishes;
  - your invites (your email invites included) and the sync change log;
  - your profile (display name and the reference to your profile photo);
  - your memberships and reservations on other people's lists, and every membership and reservation on your own lists;
  - finally the user account itself, together with its sign-in sessions and linked Google/Facebook identities.

  This data is not kept marked as deleted; it is actually removed. If deleting the account fails after the photos were deleted, some photos may already be gone; the account deletion can then be tried again.
- **On the device you delete from:** the app removes the account's local data and photos, reminder settings and pending reminders, then signs you out. If the app stops part way, it finishes on its next start.
- **On the account's other devices**, the data is **not deleted automatically**. Those devices will eventually be signed out, so the data is no longer available through the app in the normal way, but it may remain in their local database. You can remove it there by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
- Email invites that other users sent to your email address belong to their accounts, so deleting your account does not delete them (see section 11).
- If another of your devices, still holding a valid sign-in, uploads a photo while the account is being deleted, that photo may remain in the file storage. No other user can access such a leftover photo, and we delete it through manual maintenance.
- For backups, see section 11.

**By email, without the app:** if you cannot access the app or do not want to reinstall it, you can request deletion by email:

- Write to support@giftiary.app, preferably from the email address you use with Giftiary, and say that you want your account deleted.
- We then send a confirmation email to the account's email address. We delete the account once you reply to that email from that address and confirm the request. This is how we check that the account is really yours.
- If you are not sure which email address belongs to your account (with Google or Facebook sign-in, it is the address received from the provider), tell us which sign-in method you used. We always send the confirmation to the account's email address; that is the only way we can verify a request by email.
- By default we do not ask for a password, verification code or other sign-in credentials, or for an identity document. Never send us any of these.
- The time limit runs from when your request reaches us. We send the confirmation email promptly after receiving your request, delete the account without undue delay once you confirm, and tell you what we have done within one month of receiving your request. Your confirmation does not start a new time limit. If we cannot act on the request (for example, because no confirmation arrives from the account's address), we tell you so, with the reason, within the same time limit. On the server, the same is deleted as with deletion in the app (including photos); we cannot delete the local data on your devices remotely, which you can remove by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. We will let you know by email once the deletion is done.

Account deletion steps and details: https://giftiary.app/account-deletion/

## 13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- request information about, and **access** to, the data we process about you;
- request **rectification** of inaccurate data (you can change most data in the app yourself);
- request **erasure** of your data (see section 12);
- request **restriction** of processing;
- **data portability** (to receive your data in a machine-readable format);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest.

Send your request to support@giftiary.app. We answer without undue delay, and within one month of receiving your request at the latest. Before acting on a request, we may check that the account really belongs to you.

You can lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, https://naih.hu), with the supervisory authority of your place of residence in the EU, or go to court.

## 14. Security

- The connection between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Every account can access only its own data; database-level access control enforces this.
- Passwords and invite codes are stored only in an irreversible (hashed) form.
- Photos are in private file storage: every account can access only its own folder, there are no public links, and profile photos are kept apart from all other photos.
- Person and profile photos are never exposed through wishlist sharing or invites; the server does not return them to anyone but their owner.
- The owner of a shared list does not receive reservations, not even through the server.
- Android backup does not include the sign-in session, the local database or photos.

No system can guarantee complete security, but we protect your data with reasonable measures.

## 15. Children

Giftiary is currently intended for adults and is not directed to children. If you believe a child's data has been entered into the service, please let us know.

## 16. What we do not do

- We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and we do not track your activity in other apps or on websites.
- We do not request access to location, contacts, camera, photos or files; a photo is made only from the image you choose or take with your camera app.
- We do not process payment data.
- We do not sell your data.
